3. Read the mini dialogues

Read with your teacher. Then swap roles.

At reception

Receptionist: Hello.

Guest: Hello. I have a reservation.

Receptionist: Your name, please?

Guest: My name is Tom.

Receptionist: Passport, please.

Guest: Here you are.

A room

Receptionist: Single or double?

Guest: I’d like a single room.

Receptionist: OK. Here is your key.

Guest: Thank you.

Questions

Guest: What time is breakfast?

Receptionist: At 7.

Guest: Is there Wi-Fi?

Receptionist: Yes, there is.

Guest: Can I have a towel, please?

Receptionist: Yes. Here you are.
